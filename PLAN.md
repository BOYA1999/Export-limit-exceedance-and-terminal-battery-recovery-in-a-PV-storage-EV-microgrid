Question: under a shared local energy/service-priority QP, how do learned, rule-based and predictive proposals trade imported operating scores against export exceedance and terminal recovery? The correction penalty may yield small or inconsistent improvements; baseline constraints and rule choices may explain prior advantages. These are empirical alternatives, not assumed positive results.
